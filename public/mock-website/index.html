Questions

Do I need a finished project to show?

No. Works in progress are welcome.

Where is it?

Studio Bell, National Music Centre, 850 4 St SE, Calgary. Come in through the north doors on 4 Street, beside the NMC Café.

Which parts can I attend?

The night market and the film festival are open with an RSVP. The dinner is invite only.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes. Each person should RSVP so we can plan for the room.

Can I sell things at the market?

Yes. Vendors set up in the Soundscapes Gallery. Food must be prepacked, with no open food stations.

Is there an age limit?

To be confirmed.

What should I wear?

Whatever you would wear to a gallery opening.

I'm not in tech. Is this for me?

Yes. Painters, filmmakers, musicians, designers and small business owners are exactly who this is for.
